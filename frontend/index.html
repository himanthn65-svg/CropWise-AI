THE CHALLENGE

Choosing the right crop isn't always simple.

Crop selection can depend on many interacting factors. Soil nutrients, temperature, humidity, pH and rainfall can all influence which crops are more suitable for a particular set of conditions.

Instead of looking at these factors separately, CropWise AI brings them together and uses a trained machine learning model to identify a suitable crop from the available possibilities.
